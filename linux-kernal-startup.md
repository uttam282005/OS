## How the boot process works
- Power on the computer.
- The computer's firmware (BIOS or UEFI) runs hardware checks.
- The firmware finds and starts the boot loader on a storage device.
- The boot loader loads the operating system kernel (such as Windows, Linux, or macOS) into RAM.
- The operating system takes over and starts running.
